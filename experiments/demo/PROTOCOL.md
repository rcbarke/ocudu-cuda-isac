# Single-Spark protocol

[profile.json](profile.json) contains the demonstration settings and acceptance criteria. Freeze one cell, one fixed UE, one layer, four calibrated RX channels and 51 PRBs at 30 kHz SCS. No UE, 5GC or PRACH procedure is attached: a Sionna RT radar CFR applied to a legal grant at the receiver stands in for the UE. Record the actual legal UL symbol/DMRS mask and replay cadence. Replay target-absent and target-present scenes through the same receiver stimulus and load.

Two sensing branches run. The cuSense-like branch reads the receiver's own estimate (O3, S5.2) and publishes a presence score and a location map once per TDD period. The delay–Doppler branch reads the observer's pilot LS (O2), publishes one decision per eight updates and serves as the baseline. The profile names the branch whose presence decision is graded. Fit the background template and normalization on the training split, calibrate the threshold on validation negatives and freeze everything before a held-out episode is read.

The radio allowance and the sensing deadlines in the profile start as proposals. Run S4.6 on training and validation episodes, derive the values by the declared rules, verify them on a second run and set the profile status to `calibrated` before the evidence run. A change after that is a new profile with a new hash.

Show baseline DSP, isolated sensing on captured observations, and concurrent DSP+sensing with MPS. Warm the cached traces and model and collect at least three paired 60-second repetitions of the three arms in alternating or randomized order. Run a capture-only copy control and a separate overload diagnostic. Grade each concurrent repetition on the same 20 held-out episodes. Record position error and latency beside cuSense's published figures without grading them.

Run the KPI aggregator and the page with every arm. Record page attachment and viewer location, and measure the page-off, remote and local arms once. The page's counts must equal the scorecard. See [S2–S4](../../docs/dev/DEVELOPMENT.md#phase-s2) for gates, the [advisor runbook](../../docs/demo/ADVISOR_DEMO.md) for the live sequence and the [KPI contract](../../docs/interfaces/KPI.md) for the page.
