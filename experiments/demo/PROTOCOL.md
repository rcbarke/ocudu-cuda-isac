# Single-Spark protocol

[profile.json](profile.json) contains the proposed demonstration settings and acceptance criteria. Freeze one cell, one fixed UE, one layer, four calibrated RX channels and one modest PUSCH allocation. Record the actual legal UL symbol/DMRS mask and replay cadence. Replay target-absent and target-present scenes through the same receiver stimulus and load. Show baseline DSP, isolated sensing on captured observations, and concurrent DSP+sensing with MPS. Warm the cached traces and model and collect at least three paired 60-second repetitions of the three arms in alternating or randomized order. Run a capture-only copy control and a separate overload diagnostic. Grade each concurrent repetition on the same 20 held-out episodes. See [S1–S4](../../docs/dev/DEVELOPMENT.md) for gates and [advisor runbook](../../docs/demo/ADVISOR_DEMO.md) for the live sequence.
