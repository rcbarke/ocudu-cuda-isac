## Sensing comparators

- **Delay–Doppler and CFAR.** Use Sionna's conventions to verify transform dimensions, delay/velocity signs, windowing and units. State how cell-averaging or order-statistic CFAR estimates the local reference level, how many guard and training cells it uses, and how the detection threshold is fixed on calibration data. H. Rohling's paper discusses clutter edges and interfering targets. [Sionna ISAC processing](https://nvlabs.github.io/sionna/phy/api/isac/index.html) · [Rohling, IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.1983.309350)
- **Classical MUSIC.** R. O. Schmidt, *Multiple Emitter Location and Signal Parameter Estimation*. Use known array geometry, a stated source-count rule, calibrated steering vectors and the same observation window as the competing estimators. Angle of arrival is only one observable in an uplink bistatic scene. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAP.1986.1143830)
- **Learned subspace estimators.** DA-MUSIC and SubspaceNet are existing methods for estimating directions from sensor arrays. Their authors provide source code. Select the method in advance, retain its source/version/license record and retrain or evaluate against the local aperture, coordinate convention and signal model. A DoA method needs an explicitly defined detection or localization layer before it can be scored on those tasks. [DA-MUSIC paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVT.2023.3320360) · [DA-MUSIC code](https://github.com/julianmer/DA-MUSIC_TVT23) · [SubspaceNet paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVT.2024.3496119) · [SubspaceNet code](https://github.com/ShlezingerLab/SubspaceNet)
- **Deferred.** The plan covers baseline MUSIC (S6.3), with its native kernels in S6.13. Further variants, such as forward–backward averaging, spatial smoothing, root-MUSIC, the approximate decomposition of MUSIC-lite and the MILCOM 2026 work, are not planned. The angle registry can take them later as rows. [MUSIC-lite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04849v1)
- **Radar chain named by the internship description.** NVIDIA's Aerial ISAC applied-research internship (JR2028027) lists radar cube formation, range and Doppler processing, angle estimation, CFAR detection and clutter suppression by MTI, static clutter removal and adaptive filtering, with new model architectures and loss functions for sensing and joint sensing and communication tasks. The [requirements table](../dev/DEVELOPMENT.md#requirements-and-where-they-are-built) maps each item to a ticket.
